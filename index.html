About me

Coaching shaped by two decades inside organisations.

I've spent more than 20 years working in commercial businesses as a senior HR professional. Along the way I've supported many people through restructures, promotions, new roles and difficult periods at work, and seen what helps people move forward with confidence.

I now bring that experience to one-to-one coaching. I understand how organisations work, what hiring managers look for and the pressures people carry at work. My coaching is practical and supportive, and we work towards goals you set.

I work with clients across Milton Keynes and Bedford, in person or online.

How coaching works

A clear, supportive process.

Coaching is a confidential, one-to-one partnership. I won't tell you what to do. I'll help you think clearly, see your options and commit to action.

  1. Free consultation

    A relaxed 30-minute call by phone or video to talk about where you are and whether we're a good fit.

  2. Set your goals

    We agree what success looks like for you and the right number of sessions.

  3. Coaching sessions

    Focused sessions, online or in person, with practical actions to take between them.

  4. Move forward

    We review your progress and make sure you leave with the clarity and confidence to keep going.

    Questions

    Frequently asked questions

    What happens in the free consultation?

    It's an informal 30-minute conversation by phone or video. We'll talk about what's going on for you and what you'd like to change, and you can ask any questions about how coaching works. There's no pressure to book.

    Do you coach online or in person?

    Both. I see clients in person in Milton Keynes (MK7) and Bedford (MK40), and online by video call from anywhere.

    How many sessions will I need?

    It depends on your goals. Some people get what they need from one focused session. Others benefit from a programme of three to six sessions. We'll agree this together in your consultation.

    How do I pay?

    You can book and pay online by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through Stripe's secure checkout. You'll get a receipt by email straight away, and I'll be in touch within two working days to arrange your first session. Packages are paid in full when you book.

    Can my employer pay for coaching?

    Yes. Many organisations fund coaching for their people. I can provide an invoice and agree the arrangement with you and your employer up front.

    Is coaching confidential?

    Yes. As an EMCC professional member I follow the Global Code of Ethics for coaches and mentors, and everything we discuss stays between us.

    What is your cancellation policy?

    Please give at least 48 hours' notice to rearrange a session. Sessions cancelled with less notice, or missed, count as used. You can cancel a booking within 14 days for a refund. See the coaching terms for details.
